### 📋 QUESTION

**The four sentences (labelled 1, 2, 3, 4) below, when properly sequenced would yield a coherent paragraph. Decide on the proper sequencing of the order of the sentences and enter the sequence of the four numbers as your answer.**

1. What appears to influence behavior more decisively is the certainty that wrongdoing will be detected and promptly penalized, creating a clear and immediate connection between action and outcome.
2. Research on crime deterrence consistently indicates that the harshness of punishment, such as longer prison sentences, plays a far smaller role than the likelihood and speed with which consequences are enforced.
3. The effect is particularly pronounced among teenagers, whose heightened impulsivity and tendency to prioritize short-term rewards make them less responsive to distant or abstract threats of severe punishment.
4. The principle applies broadly across age groups, suggesting that predictability in enforcement fosters stronger behavioral restraint than severity alone.

---

### 📚 SUBJECT & TOPIC
- **Subject:** Verbal Ability / English Linguistics
- **Topic:** Sentence Ordering, Discourse Architecture & Reference Resolution

---

### 💡 CONCEPT

To solve high-level parajumble questions, two key discourse principles must be applied:

1. **Anaphoric & Nominal Reference Resolution:** Sentences containing noun phrases with definite articles or demonstrative pronouns (e.g., *"The principle"*, *"The effect"*) require prior established concepts as antecedents.
2. **General-to-Specific Structural Progression:** Logical expository prose moves from a general rule/finding applicable across a broad spectrum (*"broadly across age groups"*) to specific sub-cases or demographic examples (*"particularly pronounced among teenagers"*).

---

### 🔗 SENTENCE RELATIONSHIPS & REFERENCES

- **Mandatory Opening (Sentence 2):** Sentence 2 acts as the overarching thesis statement, introducing foundational research on crime deterrence comparing severity (*harshness*) versus certainty/speed (*likelihood and speed*).
- **Elaboration Link (Sentence 2 $\rightarrow$ Sentence 1):** Sentence 1 expands upon the finding in Sentence 2, defining the exact mechanism (*certainty of detection and prompt penalty*).
- **Synthesizing Reference (Sentence 1 $\rightarrow$ Sentence 4):** Sentence 4 introduces *"The principle"*, summarizing the core finding established in Sentences 2 and 1 (*predictability vs. severity*) and stating its general scope (*"broadly across age groups"*).
- **Demographic Narrowing (Sentence 4 $\rightarrow$ Sentence 3):** Sentence 3 uses *"The effect"* to narrow down the broad statement in Sentence 4 (*"across age groups"*) to a specific demographic group (*"teenagers"*).

---

### 📝 STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION

1. **Identify the Independent Opening Sentence:**
   - Sentences 3 and 4 begin with reference markers (*"The effect"*, *"The principle"*), ruling them out as paragraph openers.
   - Sentence 1 begins with a relative clause (*"What appears to influence..."*) that logically responds to a context set up by research.
   - **Sentence 2** introduces the central topic independently: *"Research on crime deterrence consistently indicates..."* $\rightarrow$ **Position 1 = 2**.

2. **Establish the Direct Continuation (2 $\rightarrow$ 1):**
   - Sentence 2 contrasts the *harshness* of punishment against the *likelihood and speed* of enforcement.
   - Sentence 1 elaborates directly on this contrast: *"What appears to influence behavior more decisively is the certainty..."* $\rightarrow$ **Position 2 = 1**.

3. **Establish Scope and Reference (1 $\rightarrow$ 4):**
   - Sentence 4 synthesizes the idea established in 2 and 1, labeling it *"The principle"* (*predictability in enforcement fosters stronger restraint than severity alone*). It asserts that this rule applies *"broadly across age groups"* $\rightarrow$ **Position 3 = 4**.

4. **Apply General-to-Specific Progression (4 $\rightarrow$ 3):**
   - Sentence 3 refers to *"The effect"* and focuses specifically on **teenagers**, serving as a concrete demographic illustration of the broad rule mentioned in Sentence 4 $\rightarrow$ **Position 4 = 3**.

### 🎯 FINAL ANSWER

**2143**

### 🌟 SIMPLE EXPLANATION

- **Sentence 2** introduces the main research finding: severe punishments are less effective than speedy, likely ones.
- **Sentence 1** explains *why*: certainty and swiftness create a clear, immediate link between action and consequence.
- **Sentence 4** labels this insight as a general rule ("The principle") that applies across all age groups.
- **Sentence 3** highlights a specific example ("teenagers"), showing where this rule is most strongly observed.

---

### ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKE

- **Placing 3 before 4 (Sequence 2134):** 
  Placing teenagers before the general mention of "across age groups" breaks the standard expository flow. Logical writing moves from general principles to specific cases, not vice versa.

---

### 📌 KEY TAKEAWAY

When sequencing paragraphs discussing demographics or scope, always follow the hierarchy:
$$\text{Core Research Finding} \longrightarrow \text{General Scope ("all age groups")} \longrightarrow \text{Specific Subgroup ("teenagers")}$$
